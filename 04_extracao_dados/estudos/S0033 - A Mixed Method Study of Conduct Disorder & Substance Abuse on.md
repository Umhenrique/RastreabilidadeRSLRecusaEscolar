## Resumo (Abstract)
> [!ABSTRACT] Resumo do Artigo
> In India, the delinquent behaviour of children is a significant issue that threatens the well-being and safety of many young people. This research examines the effect of conduct disorder and substance abuse on children's behavior. A descriptive cross-sectional survey was carried out with 53 boys in conflict with the law residing in both Observation Homes and Special Homes set up by the Government of Tamil Nadu, India. Results indicated that almost every child in conflict with the law due to juvenile crimes and delinquent behaviour was generally a school dropout. Before reaching 12 years of age, most of these children had experimented with alcohol and started using illicit drugs. Substance abuse and conduct disorder are interlinked and play a significant role in the delinquent behaviour of children. Early identification of youth/children at risk for conduct disorder and school absenteeism or dropping out of school is critical in preventing such problem behaviour and adopting timely intervention measures. © NAJP.
